## CDK Patterns
See `docs/cdk-well-architected.md` for:
Operational Excellence: Run and monitor systems effectively, and keep improving processes and procedures.
Security: Protect data, systems, and assets through identity management, encryption, and detective controls.
Reliability: Recover from failures, scale to meet demand, and keep workloads running as intended.
Performance Efficiency: Use computing resources efficiently and keep doing so as demand and technology change.
Cost Optimization: Deliver business value at the lowest price point by avoiding unnecessary spend.
Sustainability: Minimize the environmental impact of running cloud workloads.
